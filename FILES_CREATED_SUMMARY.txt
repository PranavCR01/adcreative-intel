================================================================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
================================================================================

"Could not auto-find data"
  → Set DATA_PATH manually in audit_and_fix.py

"CUDA out of memory"
  → Reduce batch size: --batch_size 32 or --batch_size 16

"File not found: image_path"
  → Update Config.image_col to your actual image column name
  → Verify image paths in CSV are accessible

Training is slow
  → Ensure GPU is available (check check_setup.py output)
  → Reduce epochs for quick test: --epochs 10
